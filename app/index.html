People and prosperity

Population

Young, urbanising and concentrated on Java

Indonesia remains demographically young: 68.3% are aged 15-64 and the median age is 30.4 years (BPS 2024).

Economy

Steady growth, led by manufacturing

GDP grew 5.03% in 2024 after the 2020 contraction, while manufacturing contributed 18.9% of GDP (BPS 2024).

Health & Education

Longer lives and near-universal primary school

Life expectancy reached 74.2 years in 2024 (BPS 2024), and primary net enrollment was 97.9% in 2023 (BPS 2023).

Geography & Climate

An equatorial archipelago of volcanoes and rain

Indonesia spans 1.91 million km² of land (World Bank 2023) and more than 17,000 islands (BPS 2024), with a wet season centred on January in Jakarta (BMKG climate normals).
